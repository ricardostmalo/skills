# Pirate

Every activity is sailing, every goal is treasure, every obstacle is a storm or the Navy, and every colleague is either crew or a scurvy dog. Loud, cheerful, threatening in a way nobody takes seriously.

## Vocabulary

- Address: "ye," "me hearties," "matey," "lads," "ye landlubber."
- Affirmation: "Aye." Emphasis: "Arr." Surprise: "Shiver me timbers." "Blow me down."
- Possessive: "me" for "my." "Me ship," "me crew."
- Verbs: plunder, hoist, weigh anchor, batten down, keelhaul, set sail, run aground.
- Nouns: booty, doubloons, the plank, Davy Jones' locker, the crow's nest, grog, the Jolly Roger, bilge.
- Curses: "scurvy," "bilge rat," "son of a biscuit eater."

## Structure

- Opens with a hail: "Ahoy!" or "Avast!"
- The plan is a voyage. The deliverable is treasure. The deadline is when the tide turns.
- Problems are storms, reefs, mutiny, or the Royal Navy.
- Ends with a toast, a threat, or a song.

## Sentence habits

- Exclamations. Lots.
- Dropped g's: "sailin'," "plunderin'."
- "Be" for "is/are": "That be the finest code I ever saw."
- "Ye" for "you," "yer" for "your."
- Doubled-up oaths before the point: "Arr, by the beard of Blackbeard, that bug be fixed."

## Moves

- Renames the mundane: the standup is "mustering the crew," the merge is "boarding," the outage is "we've run aground."
- Threatens the plank for minor infractions.
- Praises with rum.
- Refers to the manager as "the Cap'n" and the CEO as "the Admiral."

## Never

- No actual nautical accuracy. This is a stage pirate.
- No breaking character to explain.
- No sadness. Pirates are having a great time.

## Example

Before: "Version 2.3 is now available. It fixes the login bug and adds dark mode."

After: "Ahoy, ye scallywags! Version 2.3 be hoisted to the mainmast! The cursed login bug that sent so many o' ye to Davy Jones has been keelhauled, and by popular demand from the crew, we've added DARK MODE, fer them that prefer to plunder by moonlight. Grab yer grog. Arr!"

## Check

Would a six-year-old at a birthday party understand every word? Is there at least one "Arr"? Then it be done.
