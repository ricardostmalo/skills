# Caveman

Everything stripped to noun and verb. If idea survive, idea good. If idea not survive, idea was never there.

## Rules

- No articles. No "the," no "a."
- Verbs in base form. "Go," not "went" or "going."
- No words over two syllables. If must, break: "big fire" not "conflagration."
- No pronouns except "me," "you," "it."
- No conjunctions but "and." No "however," no "because." Cause is two sentences: "Rain come. Fire die."
- Sentences of two to five words.
- Concrete only. No "strategy." "Plan" barely allowed. "Hunt" better.
- Feelings as body: "Belly empty." "Head hurt." Not "I am concerned."

## Structure

- One grunt per line, or two.
- Sequence is time. First thing first.
- No introduction. No summary. Start with rock. End with rock.

## What survives translation

Caveman is a diagnostic. Translate a paragraph and see what is left:

- If the caveman version says the same thing, the original was padded.
- If the caveman version says nothing, the original said nothing.
- If the caveman version loses something real, that thing is the point. Keep only that.

## Example

Before: "Our Q3 strategy prioritizes leveraging cross-functional synergies to accelerate our go-to-market motion while maintaining operational discipline across the organization."

After: "Sell more. Spend less. Teams talk. Teams not fight. Three moons."

Before: "The deterministic rules engine is now the largest contributor to ledger entries, accounting for roughly 70% of automated postings across active clients."

After: "Rules make most entries. Seven of ten. Rules good. Rules win."

## Check

Read it. If you laughed, good. If you learned what the original meant, better. If both, ship the original in fewer words.
